City Roads

Houses send cars to shops of the same color. Draw roads to connect them before the shops get overwhelmed.

  • Drag to build roads. Building over water uses a bridge.
  • Right-drag (or Erase mode) removes roads and refunds them.
  • When a shop's pins overflow, its ring fills up. A full ring ends the game.
  • Each week you get more roads and pick an upgrade.
