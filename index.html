A city builder about traffic

City Roads

A highway brings you into an empty valley. Draw streets wherever you like, paint where people should live, shop and work, and keep everyone moving. Neighbourhoods fill in by themselves. Jammed roads make people unhappy, and unhappy people leave.

Roads
Click to start a road and click again to end it; it keeps going until Esc. Curves, freehand and a three-click Grid tool are there too. Design each one: lanes in each direction, median, speed limit and driveways, or start from a preset like Freeway.
Levels
Build tunnels, bridges and high bridges. Roads only meet at junctions on the same level, and ramps form where levels change.
Zoning
Paint homes, shops, industry and parks for free. Buildings fit themselves along the roads inside the painted area. Zone colours show only in the Zoning menu or the Zones view.
Junctions
Every junction is a bottleneck. Upgrade busy ones to traffic lights or roundabouts, or route traffic around them.
Watch
The Traffic view shows jams. Red badges mark homes with terrible commutes.
Camera
Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag, Space-drag or WASD to pan.
