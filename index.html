A small city builder about streets

City Roads

A highway brings you to an empty valley. Lay out streets, paint neighbourhoods, and keep the traffic moving while the town grows into a city.

Road
Drag to lay a street. Over water it becomes a bridge.
Zones
Paint homes, shops and industry near roads. Buildings grow when there is demand (the R C I bars).
Fill block
Click inside an area enclosed by roads to paint all of it at once. Area and Brush modes paint rectangles or freehand.
Park
Paint any shape. Each park lays out its own paths, ponds, trees and flowerbeds, and raises land value nearby.
Camera
Scroll to zoom. Right-drag, Space-drag, two fingers or WASD to pan.
