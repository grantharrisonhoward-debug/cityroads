# City Roads

**[▶ Play City Roads](https://claude.ai/artifact/FLQFXTX6a4Fzii37LwmGAV)**. You can also open `index.html` in any browser. There's no build step.

A small open-ended city builder about streets, loosely inspired by *Trafficity*, *Mini Motorways*
and *Cities: Skylines*. A highway brings you into an empty valley with a river, a lake and woodland.
You decide where everything goes.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Road | Drag to lay a street (straight or L-shaped). Roads over water become bridges. |
| Homes / Shops / Industry | Paint zones. Buildings grow on zoned tiles within two tiles of a road when there is demand (the R C I bars), and level up as land value rises. |
| Park | Paint any shape. Each park generates its own layout: paths from every road entrance to a central fountain or gazebo, a pond with a promenade in bigger parks, trees, blossom trees, flowerbeds, benches and lamps. Everything grows in with a staggered animation. |
| Bulldoze | Clear roads, zones, buildings, parks and woodland. |
| Inspect | Pan the map and click a tile to see its details. |

Zones, parks and bulldozing all have three paint modes:

- **Fill block**: click inside an area enclosed by roads, water or other zones to paint all of it at once.
- **Area**: drag a rectangle.
- **Brush**: paint freely, from 1×1 up to 4×4.

## Simulation

- Residents commute by car to shops, factories, or out of town along the highway. Cars queue, slow down at junctions and back up on busy roads. The **Traffic** view shows where.
- Parks and water raise land value. Industry pollutes and busy roads are noisy, and both lower it. Higher land value lets buildings grow taller.
- Taxes come in monthly and roads, bridges and parks cost upkeep. Population milestones bring grants.
- The city autosaves in your browser. Dark mode turns the map to night, with lit windows and headlights.

## Controls

Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag, Space-drag, two fingers or WASD to pan. Keys `1`–`7` pick tools,
`Z` / `X` / `C` pick Fill / Area / Brush, `[` and `]` change the brush size, and tapping `Space` pauses.
