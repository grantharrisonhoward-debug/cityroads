# City Roads

A small browser game about connecting a growing city with roads, loosely in the spirit of
traffic and city-building puzzle games like *Trafficity* and *Mini Motorways*.

Houses spawn around coloured shops. Shops fill up with demand pins, and houses send their
cars to shops of the same colour to clear them. Draw roads to link them up, cross water
with bridges, and keep traffic flowing. If a shop stays overloaded until its ring fills,
the game ends.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

- **Drag** to build roads. Placing one on water uses a bridge.
- **Right-drag**, or turn on **Erase** (`E`), to remove roads. They're refunded.
- `Space` pauses, `F` changes speed (1×/2×/3×).
- Every week (60 s) you get +10 roads and choose an upgrade: more roads, bridges, faster cars,
  or an extra car per house.
